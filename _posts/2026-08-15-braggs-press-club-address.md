## Key points

- Regulation and corruption are major obstacles to housing supply.
- Institutional build‑to‑rent can increase supply.
- **Tax policy:** While the removal of negative gearing and changes to the CGT discount increase the tax burden for investors, new builds are unaffected, and these tax breaks **would serve** primarily to drive up demand, not supply.
